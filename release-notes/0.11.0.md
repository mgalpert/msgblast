- Control msgblast from your iPhone: read replies and send to your agents from anywhere on your network or Tailscale tailnet.
- Let Claude Code, Codex, Grok Bot and other agents send through msgblast, each approved as Read only or Full access.
- Alexa Shopping Web is a new agent, with product photos on the iPhone.
- Pair a phone from the Phone section at the bottom of the sidebar or in Settings › Remote.
- Outside agents can list your agents, send or blast a message, and wait for the replies. Add them from Settings › Outside agents.
- Alexa's product results come through as one line per product with its price, rating and link.
- The Mac stays the only place that runs agents or sends iMessages; the phone is a secure remote with its own paired key.
- Pairing a phone turns on Send iMessages from the phone, so what you send from the phone to a Messages contact goes out from the Mac as a real iMessage. Turn it off any time in Settings › Remote.
- Keep Mac Awake from the phone holds the Mac awake while you are using the remote.
- Grok Bot connects over a private Tailscale https address, and Settings › Outside agents has Add buttons for Claude Code and Codex.
- Settings has a new layout, and permissions are simpler to finish: msgblast comes back to the front after you grant Accessibility.
- ChatGPT and Dots work again with the current ChatGPT app.
- Long Dots chats, app prompts that cover the message box, and scrolling and link cards in ended conversations are fixed.
- Open in Messages and Check in Messages open the person's existing chat again on macOS 27.2, instead of an empty New Message window.

Remote and Outside agents are off until you turn them on. A Full access agent can send to your Messages contacts, so approve only agents you trust. Pairing shows a QR code and a key fingerprint to compare; one phone is paired at a time, and Forget removes it. The phone app is not on TestFlight yet. Until it is, build it onto your own iPhone from source with Xcode; see [Run it on your own iPhone](https://github.com/mgalpert/msgblast/blob/main/docs/build-from-source.md#run-it-on-your-own-iphone). It needs this version or newer on the Mac.
