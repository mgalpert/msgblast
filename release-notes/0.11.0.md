- Control msgblast from your iPhone: pair a phone from the Phone section at the bottom of the sidebar or in Settings › Remote, then read replies and send to your agents from anywhere on your network or Tailscale tailnet.
- The Mac stays the only place that runs agents or sends iMessages; the phone is a secure remote with its own paired key.
- Keep Mac Awake from the phone holds the Mac awake while you are using the remote.
- ChatGPT and Dots work again with the current ChatGPT app.
- Long Dots chats, app prompts that cover the message box, and scrolling and link cards in ended conversations are fixed.
- Open in Messages and Check in Messages open the person's existing chat again on macOS 27.2, instead of an empty New Message window.

Remote is off until you turn it on. Pairing shows a QR code and a key fingerprint to compare; one phone is paired at a time, and Forget removes it. The phone app is not on TestFlight yet. Until it is, build it onto your own iPhone from source with Xcode; see [Run it on your own iPhone](https://github.com/mgalpert/msgblast/blob/main/docs/build-from-source.md#run-it-on-your-own-iphone). It needs this version or newer on the Mac.
