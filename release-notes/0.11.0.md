- Control msgblast from your iPhone: pair a phone in Settings › Remote, then read replies and send to your agents from anywhere on your network or Tailscale tailnet.
- The Mac stays the only place that runs agents or sends iMessages; the phone is a secure remote with its own paired key.
- Keep Mac Awake from the phone holds the Mac awake while you are using the remote.

Remote is off until you turn it on. Pairing shows a QR code and a key fingerprint to compare; one phone is paired at a time, and Forget removes it. The phone app is distributed separately through TestFlight and needs this version or newer on the Mac.
