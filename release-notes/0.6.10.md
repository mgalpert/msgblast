- Add rabbit OS3 as a featured agent, with its ongoing account conversation and drafts retained across comparisons.
- Import existing Chrome or Safari logins during website setup, with normal sign-in available when a session cannot be imported.
- Read comparison reports as shared findings, differences, and open questions, and see clear Terminal setup instructions for OpenClaw and Hermes.

Rabbit uses one conversation per account, so a new comparison retains earlier context. Unconfirmed requests are not automatically resent.

OpenClaw setup is optional: its chats and reports are not connected to msgblast yet. Hermes can generate reports from Messages replies after you configure its provider and model with `hermes setup`; choose it through Report options.
