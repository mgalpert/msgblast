# msgblast 0.6.7

- Connect the agents you already use with guided onboarding and automatic sign-in recognition.
- Set up Grok Bot and connect your Messages agents together, with exact contact suggestions and updated photos.
- Find Share Feedback in the Help menu whenever you have an idea or run into a problem.

Website setup advances as soon as login is recognized, including Muse’s main chat. Onboarding has fewer captions and no Check again button. The Messages access step explains its benefit and that you choose what to share, without an extra badge or icon.

Chat windows use your question as the title, and composers have fewer progress captions during normal sends. Send failures and interrupted requests keep their recovery controls.

Connect Instinct, Fo, Szn, and other Messages agents with one Connect selected button and a dropdown to change each contact. Instinct uses its updated contact photo, and selected contact photos appear during setup.

Onboarding includes OpenClaw and Hermes setup. Connect at least one chat-ready agent to finish; you can skip additional agents and return to them later. Existing installations keep their saved workspace.

Agent readiness is checked again before setup finishes, and delayed web replies are retained while a conversation loads.
