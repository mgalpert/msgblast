# msgblast 0.6.8

- Choose **Build a New Feature** from the app or Help menu to make msgblast work the way you want.
- Copy your idea and starter instructions into your coding agent, with links to fork or download the source.
- Follow expanded build, testing, and contribution guides to run your own copy and share a feature.

The invitation explains how to build separate Dev and Demo apps and submit a pull request with a description, screenshots, and video. Shared features are reviewed before they reach everyone.
